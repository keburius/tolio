# Tolio: features and what users love

Researched 2026-11-19. For each Tolio feature, this lists the best-known app that does the same job, what its users praise most, and whether Tolio already does it.

✅ Tolio has it · ⚠️ partly · ❌ missing

## 3. What users want across all these apps

1. **Free and local.** Maccy, Rectangle, Amphetamine and Boring Notch are loved partly because they cost nothing and nothing leaves the Mac. Tolio matches this, so lead with it.
2. **It just works and stays light.** "Must-install", "fast" and "stable" come up far more than any single feature.
3. **One shortcut, done.** For example: ⇧⌘2 and the text is copied, or a shake and the shelf appears.
4. **Doesn't get in the way.** Shelves that pop up on every drag and a menu that overwrites the clipboard are the top complaints.
5. **Sync with iPhone** is the main reason people pay (Paste, Yoink). Tolio deliberately doesn't do it. Desk Minder's link to Apple Reminders is a good offline-friendly middle ground.

## 4. Best next steps (most loved, still missing)

| Priority | Idea | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Medium | File shelf in Notch Island (reuse Cargo) | The top request for NotchNook and Boring Notch |
| Medium | GIF export and click display for recordings | CleanShot's recording favorites |
| Medium | Busiest apps in System Stats | What iStat users open it for |
| Low | Repeating Desk Minder reminders, Sticky Note checklists | Basic, often-asked extras |
