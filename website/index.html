Everything in one place

Turn on only what you need. Every shortcut can be changed in Settings.

Screenshots ⇧⌘4

Capture an area or a window, then draw, box, number steps or add text. Leave it alone and it's copied after 3 seconds.

Screen recording ⌃⌥⌘R

Record an area or the whole screen with your microphone and system sound. Pause and mute as you go.

Capture Text ⇧⌘2

Drag over any text on screen to copy it, even in images and videos. Reads QR codes and barcodes too.

Clipboard History ⌃⌥V

Everything you copied, with picture previews. Pin favourites and save text snippets you type often.

Launcher ⌃⌥Space

Open apps, files and settings, run Tolio's commands, do quick math and convert currencies.

Sticky Notes ⌃⌥⌘N

Glass notes that live on your desktop. Add one from the shortcut or by right-clicking the desktop.

Window Layouts ⌃⌥←→

Snap windows to halves, thirds or full screen with a key, or drag them to a screen edge.

Window Switcher ⌥Tab

Jump straight to any window, not just any app, with previews of each one.

Energy Mode ⌃⌥⌘A

Keep your Mac awake for an hour, until a set time, or while certain apps are running.

Lock Keyboard ⌃⌥⌘L

Lock the keyboard so you can wipe it clean without typing nonsense. Unlock with the mouse.

Shortcut cheat sheet ⌃⌥⌘/

See every menu shortcut of the app you're in, and run any of them with a click.

Text actions and stats

Select text to search, open links or translate. Show CPU, memory and disk in the menu bar.

Install in a minute

Tolio is a free hobby project, so it isn't notarized by Apple. macOS asks you to approve it once, the first time you open it.

Download

Get Tolio.dmg, open it and drag Tolio onto Applications.

Allow it once

Open Tolio. On macOS 15 or later: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On macOS 14: right-click Tolio → Open.

Pick your features

The welcome screen helps you choose features and grant the permissions they need.

Prefer Terminal?

After copying Tolio to Applications, this removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag so macOS doesn't stop it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tolio.app

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your clipboard, notes and screenshots never leave your Mac.

Nothing is sent

The only download is public currency rates from the European Central Bank and the National Bank of Georgia, and you can turn that off.

Nothing is logged

What you copy, type in notes, select or capture is never written to logs.

Nothing is lost

Files Tolio deletes, such as discarded recordings and old clipboard images, go to the Trash first.
