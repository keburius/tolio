Everything in one place

Fifteen tools that feel like part of macOS. Turn on only what you need, and change any shortcut in Settings.

Capture

Screenshots, recordings and text, straight from your screen.

Screenshots

⇧⌘4

Capture exactly what you want, mark it up in seconds, and get back to work.

  • Drag over an area, or click a window to take just that window (with or without its shadow). ⌃⌥⌘3 takes the whole screen.
  • An editor with brush, arrow, box, highlight, blur, numbered steps, text and crop, each one key away (P, A, R, H, B, N, T, C).
  • Leave it alone and it’s copied to the clipboard after 3 seconds. Start editing and it waits for you.
  • Copy, save, delete, or pin it floating above your windows, with adjustable opacity.
  • Full display sharpness at real size. Experimental scrolling capture joins long pages into one tall image.

Screen recording

⌃⌥⌘R

Record a tutorial, a bug or a quick demo without opening another app.

  • Drag an area or click for the whole screen. The area stays outlined, so you always see what’s recorded.
  • An options bar: microphone, system audio, pointer, and 30 or 60 frames per second.
  • Pause, resume and mute the microphone while recording. Paused parts are cut from the video.
  • Saved as an MP4, with system audio and microphone on separate tracks.
  • Not happy with it? Discard it to the Trash in one click.

Capture Text

⇧⌘2

Copy text you can’t select: in images, videos, screenshots, PDFs and stubborn apps.

  • Drag over any part of the screen. The text is on your clipboard a moment later.
  • Reads QR codes and barcodes, with an Open button for links.
  • Hold ⇧ when you finish selecting to add to your previous capture.
  • Keep line breaks or join them into one paragraph, and choose recognition languages.
  • Can read the text aloud. Recognition runs entirely on your Mac.

Clipboard & typing

Never lose something you copied, and type less.

Clipboard History

⌃⌥V

Everything you copy is remembered, so you can paste it again later.

  • Text, links, images and files, with picture previews.
  • Open it with the shortcut or from the menu bar. Search it, and pin favourites so they’re never removed.
  • The last 10 copies are also in the control panel: press 1–9 or 0 to paste.
  • Passwords and other items marked secret by password managers are never stored.
  • You decide how much history to keep and for how long.

Snippets

Type a short code like ;sig in any app and Tolio replaces it with your text.

  • Signatures, addresses, replies you send every day.
  • Placeholders for the date, time, your clipboard and where the cursor lands.
  • Works in every app, and never in password fields.

Text selection actions

Select text anywhere and a small bar offers what you probably want to do next.

  • Copy, Search the Web, Open Link, Translate, UPPERCASE and lowercase.
  • Works when you select with the mouse or with ⇧ and the arrow keys.
  • Search with Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Kagi. Translate uses Apple’s own translation.
  • Works even in apps that don’t share their selection, and puts your clipboard back right away.

Find & launch

One search box for apps, settings, commands and quick answers.

Launcher

⌃⌥Space

Open anything in a few keystrokes, without reaching for the mouse.

  • Apps, System Settings pages, and every Tolio command.
  • Quick answers: math (2+2), units (5 km in mi) and currencies (20 usd).
  • Learns what you open often and puts it first.
  • Currency rates come from the European Central Bank and the National Bank of Georgia, and you can switch the download off.

Shortcut cheat sheet

⌃⌥⌘/

Forgot a shortcut? See every one the current app has.

  • Press ⌘ twice and hold to peek, then let go to hide it.
  • Lists every shortcut from the app’s menus, so nothing is hidden.
  • Click an item to run it. Open it with its shortcut to get a filter field.

Windows

Tidy windows with a key, and jump straight to the one you need.

Window Layouts

⌃⌥←/→

Arrange windows side by side in an instant.

  • Halves with ⌃⌥←→↑↓, thirds with ⌃⌥D F G, two-thirds with ⌃⌥E T.
  • Maximize ⌃⌥↩, center ⌃⌥C, restore ⌃⌥⌫, move to the next display ⌃⌥⌘→.
  • Or drag a window to a screen edge: sides for halves, corners for quarters, top to maximize.
  • A layout grid from the menu bar, and every layout in the Launcher too.

Window Switcher

⌥Tab

Switch to any window, not just any app.

  • Live previews of every window, so you pick the right one at a glance.
  • Add ⇧ to go back. Use the arrows and ↩, or press Q to quit an app and W to close a window.
  • Optionally include minimized windows, hidden apps and other desktops (Spaces).

Desktop & Mac

Small helpers for the rest of your day.

Sticky Notes

⌃⌥⌘N

Glass notes that live right on your desktop.

  • Right-click an empty spot on the desktop and choose New Sticky Note.
  • Resize, move, pick a colour, and keep a note on top of other windows when you need it.
  • Saved automatically. Deleted one by mistake? Undo, or restore it later.
  • Show notes on one desktop or on every Space.

Energy Mode

⌃⌥⌘A

Keep your Mac awake during downloads, presentations and long builds.

  • For 15 or 30 minutes, 1, 2 or 5 hours, or until you turn it off.
  • Keep the display on too, if you want.
  • Stays awake automatically while apps you choose are running.

Lock Keyboard

⌃⌥⌘L

Clean your keyboard without typing gibberish into some window.

  • Blocks every key, including media and brightness keys.
  • Unlock with the mouse, or automatically after a time limit.
  • Fails safe: if anything goes wrong, your keyboard simply works again.

System Stats

Keep an eye on your Mac from the menu bar.

  • CPU, memory, network and battery, right in the menu bar.
  • Choose what to show and how often it updates.
  • Click for details, including free disk space.

Control panel

⌃⌥⌘M

Everything Tolio does, one click or key away.

  • Quick tiles for screenshots, recording, clipboard, Energy Mode and Lock Keyboard, each with its shortcut.
  • Your last 10 copies, ready to paste.
  • Fully keyboard-driven: arrows, ↩ and number keys.

Install in a minute

Tolio is a free hobby project, so it isn't notarized by Apple. macOS asks you to approve it once, the first time you open it.

Download

Get Tolio.dmg, open it and drag Tolio onto Applications.

Allow it once

Open Tolio. On macOS 15 or later: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On macOS 14: right-click Tolio → Open.

Pick your features

The welcome screen helps you choose features and grant the permissions they need.

Prefer Terminal?

After copying Tolio to Applications, this removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag so macOS doesn't stop it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tolio.app

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your clipboard, notes and screenshots never leave your Mac.

Nothing is sent

The only download is public currency rates from the European Central Bank and the National Bank of Georgia, and you can turn that off.

Nothing is logged

What you copy, type in notes, select or capture is never written to logs.

Nothing is lost

Files Tolio deletes, such as discarded recordings and old clipboard images, go to the Trash first.
