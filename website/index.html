Install in a minute

Tolio is a free hobby project, so it isn't notarized by Apple. macOS asks you to approve it once, the first time you open it.

Download

Get Tolio.dmg, open it and drag Tolio onto Applications.

Allow it once

Open Tolio. On macOS 15 or later: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On macOS 14: right-click Tolio → Open.

Pick your features

The welcome screen helps you choose features and grant the permissions they need.

Prefer Terminal?

After copying Tolio to Applications, this removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag so macOS doesn't stop it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tolio.app

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your clipboard, notes and screenshots never leave your Mac.

Nothing is sent

The only download is public currency rates from the European Central Bank and the National Bank of Georgia, and you can turn that off.

Nothing is logged

What you copy, type in notes, select or capture is never written to logs.

Nothing is lost

Files Tolio deletes, such as discarded recordings and old clipboard images, go to the Trash first.
