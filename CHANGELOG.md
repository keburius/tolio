# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- **The new welcome guide opens once for everyone upgrading from Tolio**, so you can see what's new and check your features and permissions. It starts from the features you already have on; nothing changes unless you change it.

## 2.0.0

- **Tolio is now All Set.** New name, new app icon (an A whose crossbar is a checkmark) and a new menu bar icon. Your settings, data and permissions carry over.
- **A new look everywhere.** Settings, onboarding and the control panel are redesigned: a dark window, glass icons for every feature, grouped rows and key caps for shortcuts.
  - **Overview:** which features are on at a glance, a short tour, the most loved features as cards and the rest as rows.
  - **Feature pages:** each one shows an example of what the feature does, its settings, shortcuts and permissions.
  - **Shortcuts:** click any shortcut to change it, including every Window Layouts shortcut on its own page.
  - **Permissions:** also shows Calendars, Reminders, Bluetooth and Music access.
  - **New Profile page** with the version and a link to the source.
- **New in General → Feel:** turn trackpad taps and tick sounds on or off for the whole app.
- **Control panel:** eight quick actions, what's running now (Energy Mode with a switch, your next reminder with Snooze, System Stats) and more actions. Recent copies moved to the Clipboard button.
- **Names:** Keep Awake is now **Energy Mode** and Sticky Notes are now **Desktop Notes**.
- **New website** at the same address.

Also in this release:


- **Features are grouped.** Settings, the Overview, the setup guide and the website list them under groups:
  - **Clipboard:** Clipboard History, Snippets.
  - **Windows:** Window Layouts, Window Switcher.
  - **Keyboard:** Lock Keyboard.
  - **Capture:** Screenshots & Recording, Capture Text.
  - **Files:** Drop Ring.
  - **Energy:** Energy Mode.
  - **Extras:** Sticky Notes, shortcut cheat sheet, text selection actions, System Stats.
- **New: Notch Island** (⌃⌥⌘I) in a new **Island** group. The notch becomes a live island:
  - pages in a glass tab bar under the island; pointing at the closed notch makes it swell, a click opens it
  - **Home:** the song, your next events and the focus timer on one page
  - **Music:** Now Playing for Music and Spotify (album cover with the player's badge, sound bars, scrolling titles, shuffle, audio output)
  - **Calendar:** your agenda for the next days in each calendar's color with a Join button for Zoom, Meet and Teams, beside the month with busy days dotted
  - **Focus:** focus and break timers set on a ruler (drag or scroll), a sound switch and a stopwatch
  - a camera **Mirror**
  - **Tray:** drag files to the notch to keep them, drag them out anywhere (one or all), AirDrop and Share; dropped text and images are saved as files
  - **Widgets:** timers with a scroll wheel, to-dos, a note, quick actions (Wi-Fi, Dark Mode, Keep Awake, screenshot, sticky note, display sleep), pinned Shortcuts, CPU and memory; add, move or remove them
  - two-finger swipes: down opens, up closes, sideways changes pages, or skips songs on the closed notch
  - live activities in the wings: music, timers, the next meeting, downloads, recording, reminders, charging, AirPods, plus a volume HUD
  - glass (default) or black look; on screens without a notch, a notch shape or a small handle; optionally on every display
- **New: Desk Minder** (⌃⌥⌘E) in Extras:
  - a glass bar above the Dock: stretch it to add time, or type 18:30, 6pm or 20m
  - add a title and press Return
  - when it's due, the screen fills with **Close** and **Snooze**
  - reminders survive a restart
  - optionally added to Apple Reminders so the iPhone alerts too
- **New: Drop Ring** (⌃⌥⌘D, or shake the pointer while dragging), in a new **Files** group. A ring of circles opens around the pointer:
  - folders (copy or move; never overwrites)
  - apps (open with)
  - people (attaches the files to a new message or email; nothing is sent until you press Send)
  - Shortcuts (run with the files as input)
  - Cargo (collect files, then drag them out together)
  - Trash
- **The Launcher (⌃⌥Space) is removed**, along with its math, unit and currency answers, and the "Send to Launcher" text action.
  - Tolio no longer connects to the internet at all: the currency rates were its only download.
  - "Search History…" in the clipboard list now opens the Clipboard page in Settings, which searches the whole history.
  - On first launch, the Launcher's settings are forgotten and its cached currency rates go to the Trash.

## 1.0.0 (first release)

The first version for friends.

- **Launcher** (⌃⌥Space): apps, System Settings pages, commands, math, units and currency conversion.
- **Clipboard History** (⌃⌥V) with picture previews, pinning and snippets.
- **Screenshots** (⇧⌘4): area or window, an editor (brush, boxes, numbered steps, text), and automatic copying after 3 seconds if you don't edit. Includes a one-click switch to use ⇧⌘4 instead of macOS's own shortcut.
- **Screen recording** (⌃⌥⌘R) with an options bar: microphone, system audio, pause, mute.
- **Capture Text** (⇧⌘2): copy text from anywhere on screen, including QR codes and barcodes.
- **Sticky Notes** on the desktop (⌃⌥⌘N or right-click the desktop).
- **Window Layouts**, **Window Switcher** (⌥Tab), **Energy Mode** (⌃⌥⌘A), **Lock Keyboard** (⌃⌥⌘L), **shortcut cheat sheet** (⌃⌥⌘/), **text selection actions** and **System Stats**.
- Control panel (⌃⌥⌘M), onboarding, and Settings with every shortcut editable.
- Runs on macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.
